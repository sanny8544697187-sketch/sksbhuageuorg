🌱 Our Story

KrishiGyan was born out of a simple frustration — agriculture students at BHU (Banaras Hindu University) struggled to find quality, organized study material in one place. Semester notes were scattered, previous year questions were hard to find, and quality mock tests simply didn't exist for free.

So a group of agriculture students decided to build the resource they always wished existed. KrishiGyan started as a small project and has grown into a full-featured e-learning portal used by thousands of agriculture students across India.

Our motto is simple: Quality agriculture education should be free for every student, everywhere.

🎯 Our Mission

We believe that geography, financial constraints, or access to good coaching should never determine an agriculture student's success. KrishiGyan is committed to:

  • Providing 100% free access to all study materials
  • Covering all agriculture disciplines — from Agronomy to Agricultural Engineering
  • Supporting preparation for all major exams — BHU, ICAR JRF/SRF, UPSC AF, State PSCs
  • Building a community of agriculture students who help each other grow
  • Leveraging AI technology to give every student a personal tutor
