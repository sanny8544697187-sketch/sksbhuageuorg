    🌿 About Us
    Welcome to KrishiGyan!
    We are a passionate group of agriculture students from Banaras Hindu University (BHU) on a mission to democratize agricultural education. We created this platform to provide free, high-quality semester notes, previous year question papers (PYQs), and daily quizzes for ICAR JRF and university students across India.

    Our vision is simple: By Agriculture Students, For Agriculture Students.
    🔒 Privacy Policy Tap to expand

    Last updated: August 2025

    KrishiGyan collects your name, email, and quiz/study activity to provide personalized learning. We use Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. We do not sell your data to third parties.

    We use Local Storage to save preferences and keep you logged in. No advertising cookies are used. You can clear your browser data at any time to remove stored information.

    Questions? Email: support@krishigyan.online

    ⚠️ Disclaimer Tap to expand

    All content on KrishiGyan is for educational and exam preparation purposes only. It is not professional agricultural advice.

    AI Professor responses may occasionally contain inaccuracies. Always verify important information from official textbooks and advisors.

    KrishiGyan is a student initiative and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Banaras Hindu University (BHU) or any government institution.

    Use of this platform is at your own discretion. We are not liable for any academic outcomes based on platform content.
