KrishiGyan Account Deletion

If you no longer wish to use KrishiGyan and want to permanently delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to our support team at support@krishigyan.online from the email address associated with your KrishiGyan account.

Step 2: Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".

Step 3: In the body of the email, simply state that you want your account and all associated data permanently deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your request is processed, the following data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers (Google Firebase):

  • Your personal profile information (Name, Email address)
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Your study history, daily streaks, and quiz progress
  • Any saved preferences or bookmarks

Data Retention Period

Your account deletion request will be processed within 7 business days. Once processed, no backup copies of your personal data are retained. Please note that this action cannot be undone.
